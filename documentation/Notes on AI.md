# Notes on AI

This document records how AI has supported my Holidaze project.

## AI submission statement

This assignment is done in accordance with the briefs specific rules, including the following regulations and guidance regarding cheating:

- Noroffs regulations chapter 5
- Guidelines for chapter 5

## Planning and requirements

I used an AI assistant to discuss the assignment, break it into milestones and work packages, and draft issues and validation checklists. AI also helped compare the planned features with the Noroff API and identify limitations that the interface must account for.

## Design and prototype

- AI helped develop design proposals, a style guide, a sitemap and user flows.
- AI-generated images were used as design references and mockup content.
- AI-generated images are also used in venues created by me. Images are placed in separate folder on github.
- AI-generated texts are also used in venue descriptions.

## Code and documentation

- AI rubberducking has been used throughout the project.
- AI has also been used as a benevolent tutor, explaining and guiding me, never writing any code, or giving me complete snippets, but highlighting where my code could be improved, and where I could find proper guidance and documentation.
- AI has also suggested libraries to add that help optimize my code delivery, eg. Leaflet and DOMPurify.
- AI has also helped writing JSDOCS for all functions.

## Project Management

AI helped me sort through my github-project, and make sure all issues, PR's and deliverables are neatly tied together, and also have proper validation checklists.

In my opionion one of the most usful areas, and a massive timesaver. All checklists have been manually checked by me.
